# Summer 2026 data audit

**Checked:** 14 August 2026

**Scope:** Coppa Club at The Swan at Streatley

## Source hierarchy

1. The live [Streatley drinks page](https://theswanatstreatley.co.uk/food/menu/drinks/)
   and its [venue drinks menu](https://theswanatstreatley.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Summer-26-Drinks-Menu-HIGH-110x250-WEB.pdf)
   establish the current venue range, prices, wine copy, formats, and listed draught
   ABVs.
2. Coppa Club's current [Season of Spritz menu](https://coppa.cdn.prismic.io/coppa/pdFipmP7wvLMdqz__Summer26DrinksMenuSummerofSpritzHIGHWEB.pdf)
   establishes the eight seasonal names, public ingredients, and current prices.
3. The revised Summer 2026 working sheet supplies reconstructed measures, methods,
   glassware, ice, and finishes for training. These fields are not published public
   Coppa specifications.
4. Producer pages clarify product category and packaged ABV. The physical bottle,
   keg badge, live bar bible, and till remain the service authority.

## Open service checks

- **Seasonal availability and price:** the live Streatley page directly links the
  venue/core menu, but does not independently confirm every seasonal serve at the
  Streatley till. Confirm before service.
- **Prosecco:** Canal Grando NV is the only Prosecco named on the venue wine list
  and is therefore the likely cocktail pour, but no public source maps it to each
  generic `Prosecco` ingredient.
- **Alcohol-free sparkling:** REAL Sparkling Dry White is the venue's listed option
  and is the likely AF sparkling cocktail pour, but that mapping is not public. It
  is approximately 0.5% ABV, not 0.0%.
- **Public versus working wording:** the public Espresso Martini says `coffee`
  where the working build says `espresso`; the public Floral Spritz says `Crossip
  hibiscus`, with Pure Hibiscus retained only as the likely range product.
- **Blazing Pineapple Buck:** public wording can be read as one `spiced orange ginger
  beer` mixer; the working build separates ginger, spiced-orange, and ginger beer.
  Confirm the actual dispense/build.
- **Operational details:** bitters without a quantity, small side pours, garnish,
  vintage, bottle ABV, keg ABV, and seasonal ratios remain live bar-bible or label
  checks where the public menu is silent.
- **Draught range:** the public menu lists four core lines and invites guests to
  ask for the full draught selection, so rotating taps remain a live bar check.
